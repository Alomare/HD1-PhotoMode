# Photo Mode

A photo mode for HELLDIVERS (2015), for the [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher) mod manager.

Photo Mode stops the mission, clears the HUD and hands over the camera. A panel of controls sets the lens, depth of field, filters, fog, the time of day and the sun, places up to eight lights, and poses the Helldiver. Pictures are saved to `Pictures\Helldivers`.

## How to use

Press **F7**, or pick **PHOTO MODE** in the pause menu, during a mission or on the ship. Esc or F7 leaves, and everything goes back as it was.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| W A S D, Q E | Move the camera |
| Right mouse button | Look around |
| Mouse wheel | Field of view, distance or zoom |
| Shift, Alt | Faster, slower |
| Left mouse button, twice | Focus on the point under the cursor |
| P | Take a picture |
| H | Hide or show the panel |

P, H and F7 can be changed in the Keys tab of Photo Mode in Obsidian.

## Known issues

- With other players in the lobby the game keeps running, as pausing one game would split it from the others.
- In exclusive fullscreen, pictures can come out black. Borderless or windowed mode works.
- Photo Mode does not open while Third Person Camera is installed.
